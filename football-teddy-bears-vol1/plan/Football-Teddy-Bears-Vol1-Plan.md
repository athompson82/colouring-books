# Football Teddy Bears – Volume 1: Concept & Production Plan

---

## STEP 1 – Positioning

**Recommended title:** Football Teddy Bears: Gridiron Grit
**Recommended subtitle:** A Football Coloring Book for Teens – 40 Intense Action Scenes of Athletic Teddy Bear Players

**Target audience**
- Main buyer: teens aged 13 to 18 who play or follow football, especially players aged 13 to 16.
- Second buyer: parents, grandparents and coaches buying a gift (birthdays, holidays, end-of-season team gifts).
- Third buyer: adult football fans who enjoy relaxing colouring books.

**Style direction**
- Bears look like athletes, not toys. They have broad shoulders, thick arms, a strong stance and serious eyes with some swagger. Their ears and snout are still clearly teddy bear.
- Draw them like a sports poster or a cel-shaded game. Use low camera angles, tilted framing, motion lines, flying turf and dramatic stadium lights.
- Use bold outlines with plenty of medium and small areas to fill: pad seams, cleat studs, stitching on the ball, crowd silhouettes and stadium structure. Detailed but not cluttered.
- Gear is plain and generic. Helmets have no decals, and jerseys have stripe trim with no numbers or names. Use the same character look on every page (see the character anchor in Step 4).

**How it differs from a young children's colouring book**

| Young kids' book | Football Teddy Bears (teens) |
|---|---|
| Big simple shapes, thick lines | Bold outlines plus finer interior detail |
| Cute, round, smiling bears | Muscular, focused, intense bears with real attitude |
| Static, centred poses | Dynamic angles, motion lines, collisions, perspective |
| Plain backgrounds | Stadiums, locker rooms, crowds, rain, lights |
| Silly or cuddly scenes | Real football: pass rush, stiff-arms, goal-line stands |
| Takes 5 minutes | Takes 30 to 60+ minutes per page with markers or pencils |

Cover and copy should say "for teens" and use words like *intense*, *action*, *gridiron* and *grit*. Avoid "cute", "my first" and "ages 4–8".

> **Spelling tip:** use "Coloring" (US spelling) in the Amazon title, subtitle and keywords. Most KDP search traffic comes from Amazon.com.

## STEP 4 – Page Titles, Descriptions & AI Prompts

### How to use the prompts
Each prompt below is complete and ready to copy and paste. Every prompt includes the same **character anchor** and **style tail**, so the bears and line quality stay consistent across all 40 pages.

**Character anchor (already included in each prompt):**
> athletic teddy bear football players with strong muscular builds, round bear ears, short muzzle, stylized fur edges, confident determined eyes, wearing plain unmarked football helmets with face masks, shoulder pads, plain jerseys with simple stripe trim and no numbers, football pants and cleats

**Style tail (already included in each prompt):**
> black and white coloring book page, clean bold black outlines, crisp line art, no shading, no grayscale, no color, no gradients, no hatching, pure white background, detailed composition for older teens, no text, no numbers, no letters, no logos, no branding, printable coloring book style, portrait orientation

Tips:
- Generate 3 or 4 versions of each page and keep the best one.
- If a tool adds grey tones, add "pure black line art on white, vector style" at the start of the prompt.
- If a tool adds numbers to jerseys or text to signs, add "blank jerseys, blank signs" and erase anything left over in your editor.
- If your tool has a "character reference" or "style reference" feature, use your best early page as the reference for every page after it.

## Quick Production Checklist (KDP)
- **Trim:** 8.5 x 11 in, black & white interior, white paper, **no bleed**. Keep all artwork at least 0.5 in from every edge.
- **Image size:** upscale each page to 2550 x 3300 px (300 DPI). Clean up stray grey or specks, and turn the threshold up to pure black and white.
- **Single-sided:** put each illustration on a right-hand (odd) page and leave the back blank. Total is about 80 interior pages plus front matter.
- **Front matter (3 to 4 pages):** title page, "This Book Belongs To" page, a colour test page, and a short copyright line.
- **Cover:** build it in KDP Cover Creator or Canva using KDP's cover template calculator for your final page count (spine width depends on page count).
- **Review every page for:** any numbers or letters on jerseys or signs, logo-like shapes, extra fingers or limbs, and broken outlines. Regenerate or erase as needed.
- **AI disclosure:** when you publish, KDP will ask whether the content is AI-generated. Answer **yes** for the images.
- **Before launch:** order 1 author proof copy and test it with markers to check bleed-through and line weight.
