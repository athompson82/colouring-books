# Sports Teddy Bears – Series Plan (Hockey, Soccer, Baseball)

Same as Football Vol. 1: 8.5 × 11 in, 40 single-sided pages, teens & adults, Style C bears, imprint **Cedar & Ink Press**, small "Sports Teddy Bears" banner on each cover.
Mood mix per book ≈ 13 intense / 14 focused / 13 happy. No league, team, player, brand or trophy likenesses (no NHL/FIFA/MLB, no Stanley Cup shape, no Zamboni name).

| Book | Title | Subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| Hockey | Hockey Teddy Bears: Breakaway | A Hockey Coloring Book for Teens & Adults |
| Soccer | Soccer Teddy Bears: Golden Goal | A Soccer Coloring Book for Teens & Adults |
| Baseball | Baseball Teddy Bears: Grand Slam | A Baseball Coloring Book for Teens & Adults |

**Gear lines used in prompts**
- Hockey: plain hockey helmet with cage, plain hockey jersey with stripe trim, hockey gloves, padded hockey pants, socks over shin guards, ice skates, taped hockey stick, puck. Goalies: plain goalie mask, leg pads, blocker, catching glove, chest protector.
- Soccer: plain soccer kit, short-sleeve jersey with stripe trim, shorts, long socks over shin guards, soccer cleats. Goalkeepers: long-sleeve jersey, padded gloves.
- Baseball: plain baseball cap or batting helmet with ear flap, button-up jersey with piping, baseball pants, belt, socks, cleats, leather glove. Catchers: mask, chest protector, shin guards.

Legend: **I** = intense, **F** = focused/confident, **H** = happy. ★ = in style check.
